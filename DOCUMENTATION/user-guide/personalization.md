# Personalization & everyday UX

ReView adapts to how you work. Most of these settings live in your **Profile** or behind
right-click / keyboard shortcuts — the interface stays uncluttered.

## Display preferences

Open **Profile → Display**:

- **Theme** — *System* (follows your OS light/dark setting), *Light*, or *Dark*.
- **Density** — *Comfortable* (default) or *Compact* to fit more on screen.
- **Language** — fourteen languages, English first. The interface switches instantly.

These preferences are stored locally and applied immediately, with no flash on reload.

## Keyboard shortcuts (configurable)

Press **?** anywhere to open the shortcuts cheat sheet. The **Navigation** shortcuts are
**editable**: click a key to record a new one. Your custom bindings are saved to your account.

- `Ctrl/⌘ + K` — command palette (global search & navigation).
- `g` then `p` / `k` / `b` — go to Projects / Kanban / Board (rebindable).

## Favorites

Right-click a project, shot or asset and choose **Pin to favorites**. Pinned items show a
star and appear in the **Favorites** section of the sidebar. Right-click again to unpin.

## Saved list views

On the **Reviews** list, set the filters you want (project, type, status, decision), then open
the **Views** menu and name the current view. Recall any saved view in one click; an active
view is highlighted. Views are saved to your account.

## Resume where you left off

The home page shows a **Resume** card with your last reviewed media and your last opened task,
so you can jump straight back in.

## What's new

The **What's new** panel (sparkle icon in the sidebar footer) lists recent product updates. A dot
signals unread entries; opening the panel marks them as read.

## Onboarding

On first sign-in, a short guided tour introduces navigation, reviewing, personalization and help.
You can skip it at any time; it won't show again.

## Enriched profile

Under **Profile → Identity** you can add a **job title**, a short **bio** and a **phone number**
in addition to your name, username and avatar.

## Browser push notifications

Under **Profile → Notifications**, enable **Browser push notifications** to receive alerts even
when ReView isn't the active tab (new comments, review decisions, published media). You can disable
them at any time. Availability depends on your browser and server configuration.
