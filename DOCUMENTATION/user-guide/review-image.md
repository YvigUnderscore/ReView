# Image review

> Updated: 2026-07-18

Image media open in the image viewer: pan/zoom navigation, overlay annotations and
version comparison.

## Viewer

- **Pan & zoom** with the mouse (wheel to zoom, drag to pan); double-click to fit.
- The delivery frame is shown with a **letterbox guide** at the aspect ratio
  resolved from the pipeline settings; annotations are anchored to that frame.
- **Lightbox** behaviors: paste an image from the clipboard into comments, resize
  the comments sidebar to taste.

## Comparison

- **A/B**: open two versions of the same task side by side.
- **Reference**: pin a reference image next to the reviewed one.

## Annotations

Drawing tools work like in video review (see
[Annotations & comments](annotations-and-comments.md)) — annotations are overlaid
on the image and listed with the comments.

## Fullscreen

The unified fullscreen keeps the header, viewer and comments panel visible; the
comparison panels have their own fullscreen control.
