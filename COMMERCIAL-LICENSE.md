# Commercial license

ReView is free software under the [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). For most studios that is
all they need: install it, run it, modify it, and — if they modify it — publish those
modifications to their users.

A commercial license exists for the cases where that last obligation is a problem.

## Do you need one?

You **do not** need a commercial license to:

- run ReView, modified or not, and charge your clients for the work you do with it;
- share media with clients through share links;
- keep your own media, projects and data private — the AGPL covers the software, never
  the content you put in it;
- publish your modifications and keep using the AGPL.

You **may** need one if:

- you want to run a modified ReView without publishing your modifications;
- you want to embed ReView in a product you distribute under your own closed terms;
- you want to resell ReView as a hosted service without the AGPL's source obligation;
- your legal department refuses copyleft licenses outright.

## What it covers

A commercial license replaces the AGPL for your organisation: same software, without the
obligation to disclose your source. Terms — scope, duration, support, updates — are agreed
case by case.

This is possible because the copyright is held by a single party, and because every
external contribution is covered by the [CLA](CLA.md).
